🟦 PROBLEM 1 — ENGINE DOES NOT START
------------------------------------------

🔹 Chunk 1 — No crank no sound

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: no crank, engine dead, self not working, chabi ghumane par kuch nahi, starter silent, harvester start nahi ho raha, main switch problem, battery disconnect, neutral switch issue
SYMPTOM: Ignition key is turned to start but engine does not rotate, no click and no starter sound, panel may be dead.
LIKELY_CAUSES: main battery switch OFF, battery terminals loose, battery completely discharged, main fuse blown, operator seat/neutral safety switch open
MECHANICAL_REASON: If battery power or safety circuits are open the starter solenoid never energizes so nothing turns.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF dash lights also OFF -> battery/main switch/fuse route; IF dash lights ON but no sound -> safety switch/ignition switch/starter relay route.
STEPS: Ensure gear/drive and header levers are in neutral; turn master battery switch ON if fitted; open battery cover and check both terminals tight and clean; check visible main fuse link near battery or starter; sit properly on seat and re-try start; operate neutral and parking levers fully to neutral and re-try.
TOOLS: spanner, cloth, torch
WARNINGS: Do not bridge starter terminals with metal tools.
VISUAL: Battery, Battery_Terminal, Main_Fuse, Ignition_Key, Neutral_Lever, Seat_Switch
PARTS: Battery, Battery_Terminal, Master_Switch, Main_Fuse_Link, Ignition_Switch, Starter_Relay, Neutral_Safety_Switch, Seat_Switch
ESCALATE_IF: All connections and fuses look normal but there is still no sound from starter.

🔹 Chunk 2 — Only click sound from starter

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: click click, self click, starter click, no crank, engine not turning, self maarne par click, chabi ghumane par click, relay awaz, tik tik
SYMPTOM: When the key is turned to start only a single or repeated click-click is heard near the starter, engine does not rotate.
LIKELY_CAUSES: weak battery, loose or corroded battery terminals, poor engine ground cable, starter solenoid contacts burnt
MECHANICAL_REASON: Low voltage or high resistance can pull the solenoid in to click but cannot provide enough current to turn the starter motor.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF lights go dim strongly when click occurs -> battery/terminal route; IF lights stay bright -> solenoid/starter internal fault route.
STEPS: Switch OFF key; inspect battery terminals for looseness, black or white corrosion; clean with cloth and tighten clamps; check ground cable from battery to chassis/engine for tightness; try starting again after cleaning; if still only click, avoid repeated cranking.
TOOLS: spanner, wire brush, cloth, torch
WARNINGS: Do not short battery terminals together.
VISUAL: Battery, Battery_Terminal, Ground_Cable, Starter_Motor, Starter_Solenoid, Warning_Lamp_Charge
PARTS: Battery, Positive_Cable, Ground_Cable, Battery_Terminal, Starter_Solenoid, Starter_Motor
ESCALATE_IF: Battery and cables are confirmed good but starter still only clicks and engine never turns.

🔹 Chunk 3 — Slow cranking with dim panel lights

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: slow cranking, engine dhire ghoomna, battery weak, battery down, starter heavy, dim light on crank, self bhaari hai
SYMPTOM: Engine rotates slowly when trying to start and dashboard lights become very dim or go off during cranking.
LIKELY_CAUSES: discharged battery, loose or corroded battery terminals, internal battery fault, high resistance cable
MECHANICAL_REASON: A weak battery or bad connection drops voltage heavily under load so the starter cannot reach required speed.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF battery old or used long without charging -> battery recharge/replace route; IF terminals hot or sparking -> cable/terminal cleaning and replacement route.
STEPS: Stop attempts; feel battery terminals carefully for warmth; clean corrosion and tighten; if available, charge battery from external charger; reduce extra electrical loads like lights and blower; attempt start again only after charge or jump-start from proper source.
TOOLS: spanner, cloth, wire brush, torch
WARNINGS: Keep flames away from battery when charging.
VISUAL: Battery, Battery_Terminal, Warning_Lamp_Charge
PARTS: Battery, Battery_Terminal, Positive_Cable, Ground_Cable, Starter_Motor
ESCALATE_IF: Battery fully charged or new but cranking still slow and cables get hot or smell.

🔹 Chunk 4 — Engine cranks but does not fire and no smoke

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: cranks but no start, engine ghoomta hai par start nahi, no exhaust smoke, diesel nahi aa raha, fuel problem, self chal raha engine nahi pakad raha
SYMPTOM: Starter turns engine at normal speed but it never starts and no smoke comes from exhaust.
LIKELY_CAUSES: fuel shut-off lever in OFF position, empty diesel tank, air in fuel line after filter change, fuel tap closed or blocked, stop cable stuck in stop position
MECHANICAL_REASON: If fuel is not reaching injection pump or injectors, combustion cannot begin and no smoke appears.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF fuel gauge or tank low -> refuel and bleed; IF tank has fuel but no priming resistance -> air leak/line problem route.
STEPS: Confirm fuel level in tank; check fuel tap at tank outlet is fully open; verify engine stop/fuel shut-off cable is in RUN position at injection pump; use hand priming pump near filter to pump until firm and any bleed screw flows bubble-free fuel; then re-try starting.
TOOLS: torch, spanner, hand priming pump if fitted, cloth
WARNINGS: Do not crank continuously for long periods; allow starter to cool.
VISUAL: Fuel_Tank, Fuel_Tap, Fuel_Filter, Hand_Primer, Injection_Pump, Exhaust_Pipe
PARTS: Fuel_Tank, Fuel_Tap, Fuel_Line, Fuel_Filter, Hand_Primer, Injection_Pump, Stop_Cable
ESCALATE_IF: Proper bleeding and stop control position are confirmed but engine still cranks with no smoke.

🔹 Chunk 5 — Engine cranks with white smoke but no start

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: white smoke cranking, safed dhuan self par, cold start problem, glow plug issue, low compression start problem, diesel aa raha par start nahi
SYMPTOM: Engine cranks, white or grey smoke comes from exhaust but engine does not pick up and start.
LIKELY_CAUSES: weak battery causing low cranking speed, low compression on cold engine, glow plug or intake heater not working (if equipped), poor injector spray
MECHANICAL_REASON: Fuel is entering cylinders but incomplete combustion at low temperature causes white smoke without sustained firing.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF smoke reduces when cranking speed drops -> battery/cranking speed route; IF heavy white smoke with knocking attempt -> compression/injector/mechanical route.
STEPS: Ensure correct grade engine oil and coolant level; try starting with fully charged battery; if fitted, confirm intake heater/glow plug indicator is working and pre-heat as recommended; avoid repeated long cranking attempts.
TOOLS: torch
WARNINGS: Do not use open flame near intake for heating.
VISUAL: Exhaust_Pipe, Warning_Lamp_Heater, Battery, Ignition_Key
PARTS: Battery, Starter_Motor, Glow_Plug_System, Intake_Heater, Injector, Compression_Components
ESCALATE_IF: White smoke and no start continue even after good battery and correct pre-heating.

🔹 Chunk 6 — Engine starts then immediately stops

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: starts and dies, start ke baad turant band, engine pakad ke turant band, fuel line mein hawa, diesel supply loose
SYMPTOM: Engine starts briefly, runs for a few seconds, then stops by itself.
LIKELY_CAUSES: air leak on fuel suction side, choked fuel filter or sediment bowl, partially closed fuel tap, blocked tank vent causing vacuum

DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF opening fuel cap gives hissing and then engine runs longer -> blocked tank vent; IF bubbles seen at transparent line/filter -> air leak route.
STEPS: Open fuel tank cap and listen for vacuum; clean or confirm tank vent hole is open; check fuel tap fully open and sediment bowl clean; drain water and dirt from bowl; replace clogged fuel filter if very dirty; bleed system after any opening.
TOOLS: spanner, cloth, screwdriver, small brush
WARNINGS: Keep diesel away from hot exhaust.
VISUAL: Fuel_Tank, Fuel_Cap, Fuel_Tap, Sediment_Bowl, Fuel_Filter, Fuel_Line, Exhaust_Pipe
PARTS: Fuel_Tank, Fuel_Cap_Vent, Fuel_Tap, Sediment_Bowl, Fuel_Filter, Fuel_Line, Injection_Pump
ESCALATE_IF: Engine still stops quickly after fuel system cleaning and vent checks.

🔹 Chunk 7 — Starter motor jam suspicion

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: starter jam, self jam ho gaya, pinion stuck, grinding sound, starter gear awaz, flywheel jam
SYMPTOM: Turning the key gives heavy clunk or grinding near starter, engine does not turn or turns with harsh noise.
LIKELY_CAUSES: starter pinion stuck in ring gear, damaged ring gear teeth, misaligned starter mounting
MECHANICAL_REASON: A jammed pinion cannot rotate freely and may lock the flywheel.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF repeated grinding at same position -> ring gear/starter alignment route; IF occasional jam but frees by slight engine movement -> pinion sticking route.
STEPS: Switch battery OFF; visually inspect starter mounting bolts for looseness; do not keep trying to crank if grinding noise persists.
TOOLS: spanner, torch
WARNINGS: Keep hands clear of starter and ring gear area.
VISUAL: Starter_Motor, Starter_Solenoid, Flywheel_Housing, Battery
PARTS: Starter_Motor, Starter_Pinion, Ring_Gear, Starter_Mounting_Bolts
ESCALATE_IF: Grinding or jamming persists even after checking mounting and electrical causes.

🔹 Chunk 8 — Safety interlock preventing start

PROBLEM: Engine does not start
TAGS: safety switch, seat switch, neutral switch, clutch switch, harvester start interlock, lever not in neutral, parking brake switch
SYMPTOM: Dash lights are ON but starter does not engage until certain levers are moved or operator sits correctly.
LIKELY_CAUSES: operator presence switch not pressed, drive lever not exactly in neutral gate, header engagement lever not in OFF, faulty neutral/safety switch
MECHANICAL_REASON: Safety interlocks open the start circuit if machine is not in safe condition.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF moving lever slightly to exact neutral allows start -> linkage/adjustment route; IF no response regardless of position -> switch/wiring route.
STEPS: Confirm drive transmission lever in full neutral; confirm header and unloading controls in OFF; sit firmly on seat; apply parking brake if required; slowly wiggle neutral lever within gate while holding key in start to see if starter catches once.
TOOLS: torch
VISUAL: Seat_Switch, Neutral_Lever, Drive_Lever, Header_Clutch_Lever, Ignition_Key
PARTS: Seat_Switch, Neutral_Switch, Header_Clutch_Switch, Start_Relay_Circuit

🟦 PROBLEM 2 — ENGINE LOW POWER / STALLS UNDER LOAD
------------------------------------------

🔹 Chunk 1 — Engine loses power in heavy crop

PROBLEM: Engine low power / stalls under load
TAGS: load pe dum kam, bhari fasal me engine dubna, pulling kam, engine rog jaata hai, harvester ruk ruk ke chalna
SYMPTOM: While entering heavy crop or climbing slight slope engine rpm drops quickly and machine feels weak.
LIKELY_CAUSES: overloading with too high forward speed, dirty fuel filter restricting supply, clogged air cleaner, partially seized brake or drive component
MECHANICAL_REASON: Excess load and restricted fuel or air reduce available torque so rpm falls under demand.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF reducing ground speed improves rpm -> overloading/field speed route; IF power still low at slow speed -> fuel/air/drag route.
STEPS: Immediately reduce ground speed and header intake; shift to lower gear or hydro setting; inspect and if needed clean air cleaner element; check fuel filter for dirt level and replace if very dirty; feel wheel hubs and final drives for abnormal heat indicating drag.
TOOLS: spanner, brush, cloth, torch
VISUAL: Engine_RPM_Gauge, Air_Cleaner, Fuel_Filter, Ground_Drive_Wheel, Brake_Rod_Adjuster
PARTS: Fuel_Filter, Fuel_Line, Air_Cleaner, Throttle_Linkage, Brake_Linkage, Final_Drive
ESCALATE_IF: Engine remains weak even with clean filters and low ground speed.

🔹 Chunk 2 — Engine stalls when header engaged

PROBLEM: Engine low power / stalls under load
TAGS: header lagane par band, threshing clutch lagate hi engine band, separator ON par dum girna, drum heavy, load jhatka
SYMPTOM: Engine runs normally without load but stalls or nearly stalls when threshing/header drive is engaged.
LIKELY_CAUSES: separator or header mechanism jammed with straw or mud, belt too tight or misaligned causing drag, seized bearing in drum or shaft
MECHANICAL_REASON: A jammed or seized driven unit suddenly adds very high torque load when clutch is engaged.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF engine pulls down sharply with vibration/noise -> mechanical jam route; IF belts smoke or smell before stall -> belt/drive drag route.
STEPS: Disengage header and separator immediately; switch OFF engine and remove key; visually inspect header, feeder house, drum and drives for accumulated straw, stones, or mud; clear blockage using wooden stick, never hands; rotate drives slowly by hand where safe to feel for tight spots.
TOOLS: wooden stick, torch, spanner
WARNINGS: Never clear blockages with engine running or key in.
VISUAL: Header, Cutter_Bar, Reel, Feeder_House, Threshing_Drum, Drive_Belt, Drive_Chain
PARTS: Header_Clutch, Threshing_Drum, Feeder_Chain, Drum_Bearing, Header_Drive_Belt
ESCALATE_IF: Any shaft or drum cannot be turned by reasonable hand force even after cleaning straw.

🔹 Chunk 3 — Engine hunts up and down under steady load

PROBLEM: Engine low power / stalls under load
TAGS: rpm upar neeche, engine hunting, governor problem, diesel supply up down, load me awaaz badalna
SYMPTOM: At constant ground speed and crop, engine rpm repeatedly rises and falls without operator change.
LIKELY_CAUSES: air bubbles in fuel line, partially blocked fuel filter, governor linkage sticking, fuel tank outlet restriction
MECHANICAL_REASON: Unsteady fuel delivery causes cyclic changes in power so governor continuously chases set speed.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF transparent fuel sections show bubbles -> air / leak route; IF filter very dirty -> filter/clog route.
STEPS: Check fuel level and refill if low; inspect visible fuel lines for wet spots or loose clamps; bleed fuel system at filter and pump; replace clogged fuel filter; lubricate external governor and throttle linkages lightly where allowed.
TOOLS: spanner, screwdriver, cloth, small oil can, torch
VISUAL: Fuel_Tank, Fuel_Line, Fuel_Filter, Injection_Pump, Throttle_Lever
PARTS: Fuel_Tank_Outlet, Fuel_Line, Fuel_Filter, Injection_Pump, Governor_Linkage
ESCALATE_IF: Engine speed continues hunting after air and filter issues are corrected.

🔹 Chunk 4 — Power loss with excessive black smoke

PROBLEM: Engine low power / stalls under load
TAGS: black smoke with low power, kala dhuan, engine ghutna, fuel rich, air kam, air cleaner choked
SYMPTOM: Under load engine gives heavy black smoke from exhaust and loses pulling ability.
LIKELY_CAUSES: clogged air cleaner, very dirty radiator/intercooler fins, over-fuelling from high rack setting, working in too high gear or speed
MECHANICAL_REASON: Lack of air with high fuelling makes mixture too rich, causing incomplete combustion and smoke with poor power.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF cleaning air cleaner reduces smoke -> air cleaner route; IF smoke still heavy at low speed and clean intake -> injection/overload route.
STEPS: Reduce ground speed immediately; inspect air cleaner pre-cleaner bowl and element and clean as per instruction; clean radiator and intercooler fins from dust with air or soft brush; avoid overloading engine by cutting too wide or too fast.
TOOLS: brush, cloth, low-pressure air if available, torch
WARNINGS: Keep hands away from fan and belt when cleaning near radiator.
VISUAL: Exhaust_Pipe, Air_Cleaner, Radiator_Fins, Fan_Belt, Engine_RPM_Gauge
PARTS: Air_Cleaner, Air_Cleaner_PreCleaner, Radiator, Intercooler_If_Fitted, Injection_Pump
ESCALATE_IF: Black smoke and low power continue even with clean intake side and moderate load.

🔹 Chunk 5 — Power loss after long running, temperature high

PROBLEM: Engine low power / stalls under load
TAGS: garam hone par dum kam, long run ke baad engine weak, overheating with power drop, coolant heat, radiator choke
SYMPTOM: After some hours of harvesting, engine temperature gauge rises and pulling power decreases.
LIKELY_CAUSES: radiator fins clogged with chaff and dust, low coolant level, fan belt loose or slipping, cooling system partially blocked

STEPS: Park safely and idle engine a few minutes; stop engine and allow to cool; inspect radiator front screen and fins and clean dust and chaff thoroughly; check fan belt tension and adjust if loose; after cooling, check coolant level and top up if low.
TOOLS: brush, air blower, spanner, torch
WARNINGS: Never open radiator cap when hot.
VISUAL: Radiator, Radiator_Cap, Radiator_Fins, Fan_Belt, Temperature_Gauge
PARTS: Radiator, Radiator_Screen, Radiator_Fins, Fan_Belt, Water_Pump, Thermostat
ESCALATE_IF: Temperature quickly rises again and power drops even with clean radiator and correct coolant level.

🟦 PROBLEM 3 — ENGINE OVERHEATING
------------------------------------------

🔹 Chunk 1 — Temperature gauge in red with normal load

PROBLEM: Engine overheating
TAGS: engine garam, temperature red, heat warning, coolant boil, pani ubal raha, overheat under normal work
SYMPTOM: Temperature gauge moves into red zone and possible warning lamp or buzzer comes even at normal harvesting load.
LIKELY_CAUSES: radiator fins clogged, radiator screen covered with chaff, low coolant, thermostat stuck closed
MECHANICAL_REASON: Blocked airflow or restricted coolant flow prevents heat from leaving engine.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF front of radiator visibly packed with dust -> cleaning route; IF coolant repeatedly low without leak seen -> internal leak/head gasket route.
STEPS: Immediately reduce load and idle engine; stop header and separator; park safely and let engine cool; clean radiator screen and fins fully; after cooling, open cap slowly and check level; top up with clean coolant or water temporarily if needed; restart and monitor gauge closely.
TOOLS: brush, air blower, torch, cloth
WARNINGS: Never open radiator cap when hot; steam can cause burns.
VISUAL: Temperature_Gauge, Radiator, Radiator_Screen, Radiator_Fins, Radiator_Cap, Warning_Lamp_Temp
PARTS: Radiator, Radiator_Screen, Radiator_Fins, Radiator_Cap, Thermostat, Water_Pump

🔹 Chunk 2 — Coolant overflow or boiling from radiator

PROBLEM: Engine overheating
TAGS: coolant ublna, radiator se pani nikalna, overflow bottle full, steam from front, boil over
SYMPTOM: Coolant or water is seen boiling and spilling out from radiator neck or overflow hose.
LIKELY_CAUSES: severe overheating due to blocked radiator or failed fan belt, radiator cap not sealing, head gasket leak pressurizing system
MECHANICAL_REASON: Excess temperature or combustion gas in coolant raises pressure and boils fluid out.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF fan belt broken or very loose -> belt replacement route; IF bubbles appear continuously with cap off at idle when cool -> internal leak route.
STEPS: Stop engine immediately; allow full cooling; inspect fan belt condition and tension; inspect radiator exterior for heavy blocking and clean; check radiator cap rubber seal for damage and replace if bad.
TOOLS: spanner, brush, torch, cloth
WARNINGS: Do not stand directly over cap when loosening after overheat.

PARTS: Radiator, Radiator_Cap, Overflow_Bottle, Fan_Belt, Cylinder_Head_Gasket

🔹 Chunk 3 — Engine overheating with low coolant and wet marks

LIKELY_CAUSES: cracked radiator hose, leaking hose clamp, radiator core leak, water pump seal leak

🔹 Chunk 4 — Overheating in dusty crop despite clean radiator

PROBLEM: Engine overheating

SYMPTOM: In very dusty crops engine temperature trends high even after recent radiator cleaning.

PARTS: Radiator_Screen, Side_Intake_Screen, Fan_Blade, Shroud

PROBLEM: Feeder house / threshing blockage
TAGS: feeder clutch not engaging, throat band ho jata, header switch trip, safety sensor, feeder shuts off
