🟦 PROBLEM 1 — PUMP DOES NOT START

🔹 Chunk 1 — Problem Overview
PROBLEM: Pump does not start
TAGS: pump not starting, motor not starting, no start, switch on but nothing, current aa raha but pump band, motor dead, chalu nahi ho raha, start nahi le raha, borewell pump not starting, electric pump not starting
SYMPTOM: Pump does not run after switch is turned ON. No water delivery starts.
LIKELY_CAUSES: no power supply, MCB off or tripped, loose wire connection, burnt fuse, low voltage, control box fault
MECHANICAL_REASON: The motor cannot rotate if electrical power does not reach the winding correctly.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF no sound and no vibration -> power/control route; IF humming only -> capacitor/low voltage/jam route; IF breaker trips immediately -> short/overload route.
STEPS: Switch OFF main supply before touching wires; check if MCB or starter is ON; inspect plug, switch, and visible wire joints; retry only after covers are closed
TOOLS: torch
WARNINGS: Do not touch bare wires with wet hands.
VISUAL: MCB_Switch, Starter_Switch, Plug_Top, Power_Cable, Control_Box
PARTS: MCB, Fuse, Starter_Switch, Plug_Top, Power_Cable, Terminal_Block, Control_Box, Motor
ESCALATE_IF: Power is present but pump remains fully dead or trips instantly again.

🔹 Chunk 2 — MCB Tripped or Starter OFF
PROBLEM: Pump does not start
TAGS: mcb down, trip ho gaya, switch niche hai, starter off, power cut, no supply, main switch off, pump switch not on, current band
SYMPTOM: Pump does not respond at all and the main breaker or motor starter handle is found in OFF or tripped position.
LIKELY_CAUSES: overload trip, short circuit event, accidental switch-off, low voltage causing starter drop
MECHANICAL_REASON: The protection device opens the circuit and stops current flow to the motor.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF MCB was OFF and pump runs after reset -> temporary trip; IF it trips again quickly -> overload/short route.
STEPS: Switch off pump command; inspect MCB/starter position; reset once firmly; start pump and observe for 10–20 seconds
TOOLS: torch
WARNINGS: Reset only once; repeated forcing can worsen damage.
VISUAL: MCB_Switch, Starter_Switch, Overload_Reset
PARTS: MCB, Motor_Starter, Overload_Relay, Start_Button, Stop_Button
ESCALATE_IF: Breaker or overload trips again immediately after one safe reset.

🔹 Chunk 3 — Plug Loose or Socket Burnt
PROBLEM: Pump does not start
TAGS: plug loose, socket garam, plug jal gaya, burnt plug, intermittent power, hilane se chalu, contact loose, switchboard issue
SYMPTOM: Pump starts only sometimes, plug feels loose, or black burn marks are visible on plug pins or socket.
LIKELY_CAUSES: loose plug fit, burnt socket contacts, overheated connection, undersized extension wire
MECHANICAL_REASON: Poor contact creates resistance heating and unstable current to the motor.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF plug wiggles and power comes/goes -> socket contact route; IF black marks or melted plastic visible -> burnt connection route.
STEPS: Switch OFF supply; remove plug; inspect pins and socket for blackening or melting; avoid using loose extension board; reconnect only if fit is tight and dry
WARNINGS: Do not use melted plug or sparking socket.
VISUAL: Plug_Top, Socket_Board, Burn_Mark, Power_Cable
PARTS: Plug_Top, Socket_Board, Extension_Cable, Power_Cable
ESCALATE_IF: Plug or socket is hot, melted, or sparking.

🔹 Chunk 4 — Fuse Blown in Pump Line
PROBLEM: Pump does not start
TAGS: fuse blown, fuse ud gaya, no power to pump, cutout fuse, starter fuse, line fuse gone
SYMPTOM: Supply is available nearby but pump starter or control box shows no output and fuse strip/wire appears broken or black.
LIKELY_CAUSES: previous short circuit, overload, loose fuse holder contact
MECHANICAL_REASON: A blown fuse opens the circuit and blocks current to the pump.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF fuse is visibly broken/black -> fuse route; IF new fuse blows again -> short circuit route.
STEPS: Switch OFF main supply; open fuse holder if externally accessible; inspect fuse element; replace only with same rating; close cover before restart
TOOLS: screwdriver, torch
WARNINGS: Never bypass fuse with random wire.
VISUAL: Fuse_Holder, Fuse_Link, Control_Box
PARTS: Fuse, Fuse_Holder, Control_Box, Terminal_Block
ESCALATE_IF: Correct fuse blows again after replacement.

🔹 Chunk 7 — Start Capacitor Failed in Surface Pump
PROBLEM: Pump does not start

PARTS: Capacitor, Capacitor_Box, Motor, Cooling_Fan_Cover

🟦 PROBLEM 17 — SUBMERSIBLE MECHANICAL & ENVIRONMENTAL FAULTS

🔹 Chunk 35 — Mechanical Seal Failure (Moisture Sensor Trip)
PROBLEM: The control panel indicates a moisture sensor alarm, or the oil in the oil chamber is milky white.

ESCALATE_IF: The motor windings have already shorted due to water ingress.

A moisture alarm or milky white oil in the inspection chamber means the lower mechanical seal has failed and water is mixing with the cooling oil. If stopped early, the motor windings are safe. Pull the pump, drain the contaminated milky oil from the oil chamber, and replace the mechanical seal assembly. Refill the chamber with the manufacturer-specified dielectric turbine oil before restarting.

🔹 Chunk 36 — Idle Pump Impeller Binding (Post-Storage)
PROBLEM: Pump was stored or unused for several months; now it hums and trips the overload relay on the first start attempt.
MACHINE_TYPE: submersible_pump
TAGS: stored pump, jam after months, rust, scale, hums, trips on start, unused pump
ESCALATE_IF:
STEPS: Turn OFF the power. When a submersible pump is left idle in water or stored without cleaning for long periods, rust, scale, or dried sludge can cement the impeller to the casing, causing a locked rotor. Do not force it by repeatedly switching the MCB ON. Pull the pump out, access the bottom suction port, and use a socket wrench on the exposed shaft nut to manually rotate the shaft back and forth. Once it breaks free and spins smoothly by hand, flush the pump with clean water and reinstall.
STEPS_HI: पावर बंद करें। जब पंप कई महीनों तक बंद रहता है या बिना साफ किए रखा जाता है, तो जंग या सूखी मिट्टी पंखी (इंपेलर) को जाम कर देती है। बार-बार MCB चालू करके जबरदस्ती न चलाएं। पंप बाहर निकालें, नीचे की तरफ से शाफ्ट के नट पर पाना (spanner) लगाकर उसे हाथ से आगे-पीछे घुमाएं। जब यह फ्री होकर घूमने लगे, तो साफ पानी से धोकर वापस लगा दें।

🟦 PROBLEM 18 — ELECTRIC MOTOR COUPLING & ALIGNMENT

🔹 Chunk 37 — Coupling Misalignment (Severe Vibration)
PROBLEM: Excessive vibration and noise coming from the coupling area between the electric motor and the pump. Rubber bushes are wearing out rapidly.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: coupling vibration, rubber bush tear, alignment, shaking, soft foot, motor and pump joint
ESCALATE_IF: The motor or pump shaft is visibly bent.
STEPS: Switch OFF power and lock out the panel. Rapid wear of coupling rubber bushes and severe vibration indicate poor shaft alignment between the motor and the pump. Check for "soft foot" (where one foot of the motor does not sit flat on the base frame). Loosen the motor mounting bolts, insert appropriate metal shims under the feet to level it, and use a dial indicator or straight edge across the coupling halves to ensure parallel and angular alignment are within 0.05mm. Retighten bolts and re-check.
STEPS_HI: पावर बंद करें। अगर मोटर और पंप के बीच की कपलिंग बहुत ज्यादा हिल रही है और रबर बुश बार-बार कट रहे हैं, तो इसका मतलब अलाइनमेंट (Alignment) सही नहीं है। चेक करें कि मोटर के चारों पैर बेस पर ठीक से टिके हैं या नहीं (Soft foot)। मोटर के बोल्ट ढीले करें, पैरों के नीचे लोहे की पत्ती (Shim) लगाकर लेवल बराबर करें। स्केल या डायल गेज से चेक करें कि दोनों शाफ्ट बिल्कुल सीध में हों। फिर बोल्ट कस दें।

🔹 Chunk 38 — Over-Greasing of Bearings (Overheating)
PROBLEM: Electric motor bearings are running extremely hot to the touch, and grease is oozing out of the seals.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: hot bearing, overgreasing, grease leaking, bearing garam, overheating
ESCALATE_IF: The bearing makes a grinding or screeching noise indicating the balls/rollers are already destroyed.
STEPS: Stop the motor. Counter-intuitively, packing too much grease into a bearing cavity causes severe overheating due to fluid friction and churning. If the bearing housing is packed 100% full, the heat cannot escape. Remove the grease relief plug at the bottom of the bearing housing and run the motor for 10-15 minutes to allow excess grease to purge out. The bearing cavity should only be 30% to 50% full. Clean any leaked grease from the motor casing.
STEPS_HI: मोटर बंद करें। अक्सर लोग सोचते हैं कि ज्यादा ग्रीस डालने से बियरिंग ठीक रहेगा, लेकिन बियरिंग में ठसाठस ग्रीस भरने से घर्षण (Friction) पैदा होता है और वह बहुत ज्यादा गर्म हो जाता है। बियरिंग हाउसिंग के नीचे लगा ग्रीस प्लग खोलें और मोटर को 10 मिनट चलाएं ताकि फालतू ग्रीस बाहर निकल जाए। बियरिंग में सिर्फ 30% से 50% ग्रीस होना चाहिए। बाहर गिरे ग्रीस को साफ कर दें।

🔹 Chunk 39 — Wrong Phase Rotation (Reverse Direction)
PROBLEM: 3-phase pump runs smoothly but delivers very low water pressure and reduced flow rate.
MACHINE_TYPE: water_pump
TAGS: reverse rotation, low pressure, ulta ghoom raha, reverse phase, low flow
ESCALATE_IF:
STEPS: Switch OFF the motor. If a 3-phase centrifugal or submersible pump runs backward, it will not break or trip immediately, but the impeller will spin in reverse. This results in only 30% to 40% of the normal water output and pressure. Open the starter panel and safely swap the position of ANY TWO of the three incoming phase wires (e.g., swap R with Y). This will reverse the magnetic field and correct the motor's rotation direction. Verify flow has returned to normal.
STEPS_HI: मोटर बंद करें। अगर 3-फेज पंप बिना आवाज किए चल रहा है लेकिन पानी बहुत कम दे रहा है, तो इसका मतलब है कि मोटर उल्टी दिशा (Reverse) में घूम रही है। स्टार्टर पैनल खोलें और अंदर आ रहे तीन फेज तारों में से किन्हीं भी दो तारों की जगह आपस में बदल दें (जैसे लाल की जगह पीला और पीले की जगह लाल)। इससे मोटर के चक्कर सीधे हो जाएंगे और पानी का प्रेशर पूरा हो जाएगा।

🔹 Chunk 40 — Minimum Submergence Requirement (Motor Overheating)
PROBLEM: Submersible pump overheats and trips the thermal overload relay after running for 20-30 minutes. Water level in the pit is very low.
MACHINE_TYPE: submersible_pump
TAGS: low water level, motor overheating, thermal trip, minimum submergence, cooling issue, pump nanga hai
ESCALATE_IF: The motor smells of burnt insulation or varnish.
STEPS: Stop the pump. Submersible motors rely entirely on the surrounding water to dissipate their internal heat. If the water level in the sump or borewell drops below the top of the motor casing (exposing the motor to air), it will rapidly overheat and trip the relay. Ensure the pump is installed deep enough. Adjust the float switches or level-controller probes so that the pump automatically shuts off BEFORE the water level falls below the motor's minimum submergence depth.
STEPS_HI: पंप बंद करें। सबमर्सिबल मोटर ठंडी रहने के लिए पूरी तरह पानी में डूबी होनी चाहिए। अगर पानी का स्तर मोटर के ऊपर से नीचे चला गया है (मोटर हवा में है), तो वह 20 मिनट में ही गर्म होकर ट्रिप मार देगी। चेक करें कि पंप काफी गहराई में लगा हो। अपने फ्लोट स्विच या सेंसर को ऐसे सेट करें कि पानी मोटर के लेवल से नीचे जाने से पहले ही पंप अपने आप बंद हो जाए।

🟦 PROBLEM 19 — ADVANCED HYDRAULICS & SEALING (WATER PUMP)

🔹 Chunk 41 — Pump Cavitation (Marble/Gravel Sound)
PROBLEM: Pump makes a loud crackling noise as if gravel or marbles are passing through it.
MACHINE_TYPE: water_pump
TAGS: crackling, marbles, gravel sound, cavitation, pitting, vibration, noise like stones
ESCALATE_IF: The pump casing shows visible hairline cracks from prolonged vibration.
STEPS: Do not ignore this noise. This is "Cavitation," caused when the pump is starved of water on the suction side, creating microscopic vacuum bubbles that implode violently against the impeller. Switch off the pump. Check the suction pipe for blockages, ensure the foot valve is fully submerged, and verify that the suction lift (depth to water) does not exceed the pump's limits. Clean the suction strainer. Operating under cavitation will rapidly destroy the brass/cast-iron impeller.
STEPS_HI: इस आवाज को नजरअंदाज न करें। यह "कैविटेशन" (Cavitation) है, जो तब होता है जब पंप को सक्शन (खींचने वाली) तरफ से पूरा पानी नहीं मिलता। अंदर वैक्यूम के बुलबुले बनते हैं और पंखी से टकराकर फूटते हैं जिससे कंकड़ जैसी आवाज आती है। पंप बंद करें। चेक करें कि सक्शन पाइप में कचरा तो नहीं है, फुट वाल्व पूरी तरह पानी में डूबा हो, और पानी बहुत ज्यादा गहराई में न चला गया हो। इस हालत में पंप चलाने से पंखी (Impeller) टूट जाएगी।

🔹 Chunk 42 — Gland Packing Leakage (Excessive)
PROBLEM: Continuous, heavy stream of water leaking from the pump shaft right behind the casing.
MACHINE_TYPE: water_pump
TAGS: shaft leak, gland leak, rope seal, continuous stream, water spraying, stuffing box
ESCALATE_IF: The shaft sleeve is deeply grooved or scored by the packing material.
STEPS: Stop the pump. A centrifugal pump uses a "gland packing" (asbestos or teflon rope) to seal the spinning shaft. It is normal for it to drip 30-40 drops per minute for cooling. However, a continuous heavy stream is a fault. Using a spanner, tighten the two gland pusher nuts evenly by half a turn each. Turn the shaft by hand to ensure it is not too tight. If the nuts are fully tightened and it still leaks, or if the packing is hard and dry, remove the old packing rings with a puller hook and install new, well-lubricated gland packing rope.
STEPS_HI: पंप बंद करें। सेंट्रीफ्यूगल पंप में शाफ्ट को लीक से बचाने के लिए "ग्लैंड डोरी" (Gland Packing) लगी होती है। इसे ठंडा रखने के लिए 1 मिनट में 30-40 बूंद पानी टपकना नॉर्मल है। लेकिन अगर पानी की धार बह रही है, तो पाने से दोनों ग्लैंड नट को आधा-आधा चक्कर बराबर कसें। हाथ से शाफ्ट घुमाकर देखें कि वह जाम न हो। अगर नट पूरे कस चुके हैं फिर भी लीकेज है, तो पुरानी डोरी निकालकर नई और चिकनी ग्लैंड डोरी (Rope) लगाएं।

🔹 Chunk 43 — Wear Ring Degradation (Loss of Head/Pressure)
PROBLEM: Pump runs smoothly with no unusual noise and normal current, but water pressure and delivery volume have dropped severely over time.
MACHINE_TYPE: water_pump
TAGS: low pressure, old pump, loss of head, slow flow, smooth running but low water, wear ring
ESCALATE_IF:
STEPS: Switch off the power. If an older pump runs perfectly smooth but fails to build pressure, the internal "wear rings" (clearance rings between the spinning impeller and the stationary casing) have likely eroded due to sand or age. This allows high-pressure water to recirculate back to the low-pressure side internally. The pump must be unbolted and dismantled. Measure the clearance between the impeller neck and the casing ring using a feeler gauge. If the clearance exceeds the manufacturer's maximum limit (usually > 0.5mm to 1mm depending on size), replace the bronze wear rings.
STEPS_HI: पावर बंद करें। अगर कोई पुराना पंप बिना आवाज किए सही चल रहा है लेकिन प्रेशर और पानी कम दे रहा है, तो अंदर के "वियर रिंग" (Wear Rings) घिस चुके हैं। इससे अंदर का पानी वापस लीक होकर प्रेशर कम कर देता है। पंप को खोलना पड़ेगा। पंखी (Impeller) और वियर रिंग के बीच का गैप चेक करें। अगर गैप बहुत ज्यादा (0.5mm से ज्यादा) हो गया है, तो नए पीतल/ब्रॉन्ज के वियर रिंग डालने होंगे।

🟦 PROBLEM 20 — ADVANCED MOTOR MECHANICAL & STARTING FAULTS

🔹 Chunk 44 — Broken Cooling Fan (TEFC Motors)
PROBLEM: Motor body becomes scalding hot; a rattling or scraping plastic noise is heard from the rear of the motor.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: rattling back, plastic noise, motor hot, rear noise, cooling fan broken, cowl noise
ESCALATE_IF: Smoke is coming from the main terminal box or windings.
STEPS: Switch off the MCB immediately. Totally Enclosed Fan Cooled (TEFC) electric motors rely on an external plastic or aluminum fan at the non-drive end. The rattling noise indicates the fan has cracked, slipped off its keyway, or is rubbing against the metal fan cover (cowl). Without this fan, the motor will rapidly overheat and burn out. Remove the 3 or 4 bolts holding the metal fan cowl. Inspect the cooling fan. If the blades are broken or the center hub is cracked, use a puller to remove it and press on a new OEM replacement fan before operating.

🔹 Chunk 45 — Bearing Replacement & Safe Handling
PROBLEM: Motor emits a loud howling, screeching, or continuous grinding noise from the shaft ends. Shaft is very stiff to turn by hand.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: howling, screeching, grinding continuous, stiff shaft, bearing failure, jam motor
ESCALATE_IF: The bearing housing on the motor end-shield has shattered or cracked.
STEPS: Do not run the motor; catastrophic bearing failure is imminent, which will allow the rotor to drop and destroy the internal stator windings. Disconnect power and decouple the motor. 🔹 Chunk 48 — Digital Panel Error "E-OV" or "E-UV"

ESCALATE_IF: A burning smell is coming from the digital display board.

🟦 PROBLEM 22 — FIRE-FIGHTING PUMP & ENGINE CONTROLS (FM/UL STANDARD)

🟦 PROBLEM 23 — SENSORS & AUTOMATION LOGIC

MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: tank overflow, pani beh raha, auto off not working, float switch, sensor stuck, sensor fail
ESCALATE_IF: Water has flooded the starter panel or electrical sockets.
STEPS: Switch the panel from "Auto" to "Manual" and turn it OFF immediately to stop the overflow. The "float switch" inside the overhead tank sends the shutoff signal when water reaches the top. 🟦 PROBLEM 24 — FIRE PUMP ENGINE CHARGING & DRIVE FAULTS

🟦 PROBLEM 25 — MOTOR TERMINAL BOX FAULTS

🔹 Chunk 53 — Burnt Star-Delta Terminal Links
PROBLEM: Motor trips on startup, and a burning smell is coming directly from the motor's top metal junction box.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: terminal box, burnt link, star delta connections, short circuit, melted terminal, motor junction box, connection jal gaya
ESCALATE_IF: The terminal block plastic is completely melted or the winding wires are fused together.

🟦 PROBLEM 26 — SUBMERSIBLE PUMP INTERNAL HYDRAULICS

🔹 Chunk 54 — Sand Guard / Sand Slinger Friction Noise
PROBLEM: Submersible pump makes a high-pitched squealing or scraping noise underwater, but water delivery is normal.
MACHINE_TYPE: submersible_pump
TAGS: sand guard, sand slinger, scraping noise, squealing underwater, rubbing sound, awaz aa rahi andar se

🟦 PROBLEM 27 — MOTOR GREASING & BEARING MAINTENANCE

🔹 Chunk 55 — Wrong Grease Type Mixing (Bearing Failure)
PROBLEM: Motor bearings run hot and noisy despite recently being greased. The purged grease looks like a thick, hard soap or liquid oil separating.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor
TAGS: wrong grease, incompatible grease, hard grease, oil separation, hot bearing after greasing, lithium polyurea mix, grease sukh gaya
ESCALATE_IF:
STEPS: Stop the motor. This occurs when incompatible types of grease (e.g., mixing Lithium-base grease with Polyurea-base grease) are pumped into the same bearing. The chemical reaction destroys the grease, turning it into hard soap and liquid oil, completely removing its lubricating properties. Do not add more grease. The motor must be opened, the bearing housing thoroughly cleaned out with solvent, and the bearings washed and repacked with only the manufacturer-recommended grease (typically a high-temperature Lithium Complex grease for motors).

🟦 PROBLEM 30 — MOTOR ANTI-CONDENSATION HEATER FAILURE

MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor

ESCALATE_IF: Megger test shows a dead short to earth (0 ohms).

🟦 PROBLEM 31 — VOLTAGE SURGE & LIGHTNING DAMAGE

🔹 Chunk 59 — Surge Arrester / MOV Failure (Lightning Strike)
PROBLEM: Control panel is completely dead or trips main breakers immediately following a thunderstorm, lightning strike, or grid power surge.
MACHINE_TYPE: electric_motor

ESCALATE_IF: The main contactor or digital board is physically charred, melted, or on fire.

SYMPTOM: User has already attempted re-priming but water still does not come, or pump quickly loses prime again after each attempt.
LIKELY_CAUSES: foot valve leaking (water drains back), suction pipe air leak at joint, suction hose cracked, pump casing damage
MECHANICAL_REASON: If the foot valve does not seal, water drains back immediately after the pump stops and even during operation air re-enters. If there is an air leak in the suction line, the vacuum is never maintained.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF prime fills casing but empties immediately -> foot valve not holding route; IF casing stays full but pump still no water -> air lock/impeller route; IF priming cap area bubbles -> suction joint air leak route.
STEPS: Switch OFF the pump. Step 1 — Check the foot valve: fill the suction pipe completely with water and watch whether it drains back within 30 seconds; if it does, the foot valve rubber flap is worn or has debris — inspect the foot valve, clean the strainer, and replace the rubber flap if damaged. Step 2 — Inspect every suction joint for air leaks: wrap a wet cloth around each union and watch for bubbling during pump operation; tighten any loose joint or replace cracked hose. Step 3 — Check for air lock: if the suction pipe has an upward bend or U-trap, air can collect there — straighten the pipe run or add a high-point vent. Re-prime only after completing these checks.
STEPS_HI: पंप बंद करें। Step 1 — फुट वाल्व जांचें: सक्शन पाइप में पूरा पानी भरें और देखें कि 30 सेकंड में वापस जाता है या नहीं — अगर जाता है तो फुट वाल्व की रबर फ्लैप खराब है, उसे साफ करें या बदलें। Step 2 — हर सक्शन जोड़ पर हवा का रिसाव जांचें: चलते हुए पंप के पास जोड़ों पर गीला कपड़ा रखें — बुलबुले आएं तो जोड़ कसें या नली बदलें। Step 3 — अगर सक्शन पाइप में कोई मोड़ है तो वहां हवा फंस सकती है — पाइप सीधा करें।
