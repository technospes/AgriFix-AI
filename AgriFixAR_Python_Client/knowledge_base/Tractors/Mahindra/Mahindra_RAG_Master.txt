🔹 Chunk 1 — No crank no lights (dead tractor)

TAGS: no crank no light, dead tractor, full bijli gone, key turn nothing, chabi ghumane par kuch nahi, dashboard dead, self bhi nahi, total current gone, engine not turning no light

WARNINGS: Do not short battery terminals with tools.

🔹 Chunk 153 — H2 hydraulic system overview

SYMPTOM: Operator/mechanic requires an understanding of the internal valves to diagnose complex lifting issues on the 575 DI.

VISUAL: Hydraulic_Control_Head (OM-5074)

🔹 Chunk 193 — Rear tyre ballasting with water — when and how
PROBLEM: Front Axle, Wheels & Tyres
TAGS: water in tyre, liquid ballast, wheel weight, tyre traction, slipping fix
SYMPTOM: Tractor lacks rear traction during heavy draft work (ploughing) despite having the correct 12 psi tyre pressure in the field. Rear wheels spin excessively.
LIKELY_CAUSES: insufficient tractor weight for the draft load
MECHANICAL_REASON: Adding weight to the rear tires forces the tread lugs deeper into the soil, maximizing the 17.30 kN drawbar pull. Tractor tires can be filled with liquid ballast up to 80% of their total volume to increase dynamic traction.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF slip is high under load and pressures are correct -> ballasting route.
STEPS: Jack up rear wheel until weight is off the ground. Rotate wheel until the valve stem is at the top (12 o'clock position). Remove valve core to deflate. Attach a water hose adapter/bleeder to the valve stem. Fill with liquid until water reaches the valve level (this equals 80% fill). Reinstall valve core and inflate air to the required 12 psi.
TOOLS: jack, water hose adapter, air compressor
WARNINGS: Never fill tires to 100% water; air space is strictly required to absorb shock and prevent tire rupture under load.
VISUAL: Rear_Tyre_Valve, Water_Hose_Adapter
PARTS: Rear_Tyre, Valve_Stem, Liquid_Ballast
ESCALATE_IF: Valve stem threads are damaged during the water filling process.

🔹 Chunk 195 — Rotavator — PTO speed, depth, slip clutch
PROBLEM: Implements & 3-Point Linkage
TAGS: rotavator setting, pto gear, rotavator vibration, slip clutch smoke, tiller depth
SYMPTOM: Rotavator bounces heavily on the ground, engine bogs down instantly, or a distinct burning smell comes from the implement PTO shaft.
LIKELY_CAUSES: extremely hard soil, PTO engaged abruptly, implement slip clutch slipping under heavy load
MECHANICAL_REASON: The rotavator requires steady 540 RPM from the PTO. If it drops into hard soil too fast, it mechanically overloads the PTO driveline. The slip clutch protects the tractor transmission by slipping, which creates heat and smoke if overloaded continuously.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF burning smell from implement shaft -> slip clutch route; IF engine bogs down -> depth/speed route.
STEPS: Use POSITION CONTROL (not Draft) to set the rotavator depth. Ensure the PTO lever is fully engaged. Slowly release the IPTO clutch lever. Use a low forward gear (L1 or L2) to allow the rotavator blades time to chop the soil without overwhelming the 31.2 kW maximum PTO power limit.
TOOLS: spanner (for adjusting slip clutch springs if needed)
WARNINGS: Never stand near spinning rotavator tines. Stop engine before clearing jams.
VISUAL: PTO_Shaft, Position_Control_Lever, Rotavator_Slip_Clutch
PARTS: PTO_Shaft, Position_Control_Lever, Implement_Input_Shaft
ESCALATE_IF: Rotavator gearbox is jammed internally or bearings are shattered.

🔹 Chunk 196 — Disc harrow — transport safety, offset adjustment
PROBLEM: Implements & 3-Point Linkage
TAGS: disc harrow transport, hydraulic isolate, sway chain tight, harrow offset
SYMPTOM: Heavy harrow swings dangerously during road transport, or the tractor pulls heavily to one side in the field.
LIKELY_CAUSES: sway chains left loose, isolating valve left open, gang offset angle too aggressive for soil type
MECHANICAL_REASON: Harrows represent a massive rear cantilever load. If the isolating valve is not locked, a hydraulic drop can crash the harrow into the tarmac. If the sway chains are loose, the lateral mass shifts the tractor's center of gravity violently.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF harrow swings -> check chains route; IF transport on road -> isolate valve route.
STEPS: For Transport: Raise the implement fully. Turn the Isolating Valve knob fully clockwise to physically lock the hydraulic lift cylinder. Tighten the lower link sway/check chains so the implement cannot move left or right. For Field Operation: Set the disc gang offset angle according to soil hardness to balance the draft pull.
TOOLS: None
WARNINGS: Transporting a heavy harrow at speed without locking the isolating valve is a severe road rollover hazard.
VISUAL: Isolating_Valve_Knob, Sway_Chains, Lower_Links
PARTS: Isolating_Valve, Lift_Linkage, Sway_Chains
ESCALATE_IF: Isolating valve fails to lock the load suspended in the air (internal seal failure).

🔹 Chunk 197 — Seed drill — ground wheel drive, calibration overview
PROBLEM: Implements & 3-Point Linkage
TAGS: seed drill calibration, seeding uneven, ground drive wheel slip, chain drive jump
SYMPTOM: Seed drill is dropping seed erratically, or leaving large unseeded gaps in the field.
LIKELY_CAUSES: ground drive wheel slipping, drive chain derailed or loose, seed tubes blocked by mud
MECHANICAL_REASON: The seed drill meters seeds mechanically based entirely on the rotation of its ground-contact wheel. If this wheel slips in soft mud, or if the drive chain jumps a sprocket, the metering flutes stop turning, stopping seed delivery.
DIAGNOSTIC_BRANCH: IF seed drops erratically -> drive chain/wheel route; IF seed stops completely -> blocked tube route.
STEPS: Stop tractor safely. Lift implement slightly. Inspect the seed drill's ground-contact wheel for mud packing. Check the tension on the seed metering drive chain. Remove seed tubes and inspect for blockages caused by damp seed or dirt clods. Check hoppers for bridging.
TOOLS: stick/rod to clear tubes, spanner
WARNINGS: Ensure tractor parking brake is firmly engaged before inspecting the rear of the seed drill.
VISUAL: Seed_Drill_Ground_Wheel, Drive_Chain, Seed_Tubes
PARTS: Ground_Wheel, Drive_Chain, Metering_Mechanism
ESCALATE_IF: Internal metering gears or flutes are physically broken or jammed by rocks.

🔹 Chunk 198 — Normal Diesel Engine Idle
PROBLEM: Engine Vibration
TAGS: vibration, shaking, idle, normal operation
SYMPTOM: The engine vibrates or shakes slightly while idling, but produces no unusual metallic knocking or smoke.

🔹 Chunk 199 — Electric Motor Capacitor Hum
PROBLEM: Motor Humming and Locked Rotor
TAGS: hum, buzzing, shaft locked, capacitor hot, water pump motor
SYMPTOM: The motor hums or buzzes, the capacitor gets very hot, and the shaft does not rotate.
MECHANICAL_REASON: A failed run capacitor prevents the motor from generating the phase shift needed to start rotation, resulting in a locked rotor condition that draws high current and creates heat.
STEPS: Disconnect main power immediately. Remove the capacitor cover. Inspect the capacitor for bulging or leaking fluid. Replace the capacitor with one of the exact same microfarad (µF) rating.
